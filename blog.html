How Matching Engines Actually Work

Most people think trading systems are about prediction. In reality, they are about execution under constraints.

Core Mechanism

Orders are matched using price-time priority. Higher bids and lower asks are executed first, while maintaining strict ordering guarantees.

System Reality

The challenge is not matching logic — it is doing it fast, consistently, and without violating fairness under high throughput.

What I Learned

Building a matching engine made it clear: systems fail at edges, not averages.
