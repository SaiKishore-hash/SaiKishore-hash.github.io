How Order Matching Engines Work

An order matching engine is the core of any exchange. It determines how buy and sell orders interact.

Core Idea

Orders are stored in an order book and matched using price-time priority. Higher bids and lower asks are matched first.

Challenges

Handling partial fills, maintaining latency constraints, and ensuring fairness are key system challenges.

Why It Matters

In real markets, matching engines operate under extreme latency constraints and must process millions of orders efficiently.
