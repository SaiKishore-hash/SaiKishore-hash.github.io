How I Built a Trading Execution System

This project helped me understand how orders flow through trading systems and how matching logic works in practice.

Problem

I wanted to simulate how buy and sell orders interact and how systems handle execution and priority.

Design

Orders were processed and matched based on price and time priority. Unmatched orders were stored for future matching.

Learnings

I learned that real systems are less about algorithms and more about handling edge cases and maintaining consistency.
